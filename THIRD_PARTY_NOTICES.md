`tools/build_bedrock_compat.py`) build on MiEx's own Bedrock translation tables.

(https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-data, MIT License), and the Bedrock
biome ids and the stair corner names from GeyserMC's mappings
(https://github.com/GeyserMC/mappings, MIT License, Copyright (c) 2019-2026 GeyserMC).
No files from either project are included; only the derived tables are.

Sky & Lighting bundles the Bare Bones sun and moon textures by RobotPants and
the Bare Bones contributors, with permission confirmed by Sparkle's creator.
The original texture files are included unchanged in `assets/sky/sun.png` and
`assets/sky/moon_phases.png`. Installing the Bare Bones resource pack is not
required. Users can optionally select artwork from their own packs. Preset thumbnails
are Blender renders of a demonstration scene using locally installed Bare Bones
assets by RobotPants and the Bare Bones contributors.

Color management includes LOOCS V0.12 by Bram Stout Productions, Copyright
2024: its reduced OCIO configuration (`assets/loocs/loocs_config.ocio`,
unchanged) and its SDR colour-rendering, display and texture-correction LUTs,
